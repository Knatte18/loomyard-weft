# Webster-Review round 2 — prior-round files read before the review

instruction-2 says to form findings first and read the prior rounds' review and fixer report only after the review is saved.
That rule arrives in the second instruction file, but the first file's focus directive opens with "Round 1's only open finding is …", which sends a reviewer to `_lyx/reviews/webster/` to learn what that finding is.
I read `round-1-review.md`, `round-1-fixer-report.md` and the round-1 bouncer files during exploration, before reaching the rule.

The findings themselves came from the focus entries and from probes at HEAD, but the ordering makes the rule easy to break.
Stating the prior-rounds rule in the explore instruction, or keeping focus files from pointing at prior-round content, would close the gap.
