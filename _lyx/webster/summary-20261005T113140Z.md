# orch-cycle-hardening: compact cycle, notice queue and adopt hardening

All 12 batches reported `done`, each committed by its fork and recorded by `record-batch`.
Final head is `85603bc6`.

- **01 context-reading** (`fe6d71c0`): `shuttleengine.ContextReading` crosses the seam.
  The `claudeengine` reader reads the transcript backwards in doubling chunks, and a compaction boundary counts as a reading.
- **02 reread-and-reset** (`1bd4fa75`): the watcher re-reads before an automatic hard or soft cycle fires and fires only if the fresh reading still meets the threshold.
  `ResetForFreshLaunch` clears the reading.
- **03 orch-config-modes** (`fdb28b38`): `cycle_mode` (`compact` default, `clear`) is added, and any `permission_mode` but `bypass` is refused with the fix named.
- **04 compact-seam** (`f3132935`): `SessionCycler.CompactSessionSequence` and `Runner.CompactSession` carry the provider's `/compact`, and a multi-line focus is refused.
- **05 compact-cycle** (`bc8f0ed9`): the watcher runs a compact cycle behind `cycle_mode`, with `PhaseCompacting`, a persisted `ReadingTurnEnd`, a timeout path and a new compact-focus stencil.
  The clear-mode tests pass unchanged.
- **06 pane-too-short** (`3e945312`): `IdleProbe{Idle, TooShort}` is added, and a too-short pane is recorded as the stuck reason that holds cycles.
- **07 notice-queue** (`12067d13`): `orchengine` gets the notice queue with idle-gated delivery from the watcher, and `PATTERN.md` gets `PATTERN-orch-pane-single-writer`.
- **08 batten-notices** (`8a585027`): `battenshed` writes one notice per episode from the Run-Shed row through an injected `Notify` seam, with a `notice_quiet_min` setting.
- **09 batten-notice-wiring** (`01869ef3`): `battencli` wires `Notify` to `orchengine.QueueNotice` with `orchcli.PrimePaths`, and wires `AttachDir`.
- **10 stencils-no-watcher** (`08cff6f7`): the orch stencils drop watcher re-arming and teach the `[batten notice]` rule.
- **11 adopt-pid-reuse** (`423a50ac`): `proc.StartTime` is added, and an adopt passes a live pid only when its recorded start time is proven different.
- **12 orch-smoke** (`85603bc6`): smoke tests cover the compact cycle, the bypass footer and an opt-in large-session adopt.
