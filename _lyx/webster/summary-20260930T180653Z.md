## What was built

- **Webster audit (cards 1–16):**
  - Audit findings have stable keys and a correctness/policy class.
  - A state.json ledger dispositions each finding once per run.
  - `record-batch` warns on policy findings and fails the batch on correctness findings.
  - Failed batches are archived, and `recover-batch` proceeds from them.
  - Unattributable reports are archived and the batch is re-driven via `begin-batch`.
  - Run exit warns, passes dispositioned findings, or demotes `done` to `stuck`.
  - `summary.md` gains an Audit warnings section.
  - Card verify commands are re-run in-process.
  - Each batch records its card set.
  - `lyx webster rebaseline` accepts a mid-run plan edit.
  - The Master stencil teaches the `batch_failed` and `report_archived` rungs.
  - The 2026-09-30 wedges are pinned with regression tests.
- **Shed goto (cards 17–22):**
  - The history-only `OutcomeGoto` outcome and its segment budget boundary.
  - `shedengine.Goto`, the generic verb body, and registration in the shed, loom and batten subtrees.
  - Loom accepts goto history, and Loom-Preflight's half-finished stop names `lyx loom goto`.
  - The missing-producer and budget-exhausted stops name goto.
- **Refusal sweeps (cards 23–27):** trailing `way forward:` clauses plus reaching tests for webster engine, webster CLI, shed, loom CLI and loom gate refusals.
- **Docs (cards 28–31):**
  - `contracts/specs/refusal-spec.md`.
  - Package docs for webster, shed and loom.
  - The ly-drive skill section.
  - The Refusal Way-Forward Invariant in `CONSTRAINTS.md`, with goto in the Shed Verb-Set list.
