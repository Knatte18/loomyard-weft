**Intent:** Build the pool's verb logic over the journal: post, reply, read, inbox, forward, log, inject queueing, the interrupt verb's checks, holds and the privilege rules (D5, D7 storage half, D8, D9, D10 checks, D11, D12, D13, D16, D18).
`seam.go` declares the delivery seam (D1): `Strand` is one hub strand (`Name`, `GUID`, `Worktree`, `Slug`, `Live`, `PanePID`), and `Delivery` lists the hub's strands (`Strands() ([]Strand, error)`, an error naming what could not be read: the worktree list or a reed state), says whether a strand is idle (`Idle`) and whether a pause covers it (`Paused`), types one line (`Send`) and sends the guarded interrupt (`Interrupt`).
It declares the sentinels the adapter maps shuttle's answers to: `ErrBusy` (held, nothing typed, retry), `ErrNotLanded` (typed, not confirmed) and `ErrNoRunningTurn`; `creelengine` imports neither `reedengine` nor a concrete shuttle engine and names no tmux command.
`Sender` is a resolved caller: its full name, whether it is a strand, a program or EXTERNAL, and for EXTERNAL the envelope creel sees (terminal, parent process name, working directory), rendered `from ly:EXTERNAL (claude, pts/4, ~/Code/…)`.
`Store` holds the folded pool, the journal writer and the clock behind one mutex; every mutating verb appends and syncs its journal line before it changes the pool and before it answers (D3).
`Store` owns the waiter registry and the wake hook, so a later card only plugs in: a waiter registers on a message id, an inject id or a holder and is answered by the verb that settles it (a reply's hand-over, a message returned undelivered, an inject's outcome, mail arriving for a holder); and `Store` exposes a wake notification per receiver, signalled on post, reply, forward, inject and release, which card 9's `Scheduler` subscribes to.
Receivers (D5): a full strand name from `Delivery.Strands`, or a program sender's name once it has posted or read; an unknown name is refused listing the names that exist; `<shortname>:EXTERNAL` as a receiver is refused naming `--wait-reply` and `--reply`; a `Strands` error is returned naming what could not be read, never as an unknown name.
`Post`: body non-empty and at most 64 KiB, else refused (the over-limit refusal names writing a file and mailing its path); stores the message for its receiver, answers its id, answers a waiter registered on that receiver as holder, and signals the receiver's wake notification.
A post or forward to an orch receiver (`agentname.MatchesRole(role, agentname.RoleOrch)`) also journals one `withheld` event naming `creel-notices` for that change of its unread set (D16).
`Reply(sender, id, text)`: addresses the original message's `From`; the caller must hold or have read the message, else refused; an id the pool no longer holds is refused; to a strand or program it is mail and signals the receiver's wake notification, as `Post` does; to an EXTERNAL sender it is handed to a waiter registered on that id in the waiter registry, else journaled `undelivered`; a reply whose receiver is an orch strand (`agentname.MatchesRole(role, agentname.RoleOrch)`) journals one `withheld` event naming `creel-notices`, as `Post` and `Forward` do.
`Read(sender, from, all)`: `read` is the caller's unread mail, `--from` filters by sender, `--all` adds read messages still retained, oldest first with envelope; it reserves the unread ones it hands out so a concurrent read by the same holder skips them; `Ack` marks them read and journals `read`; `Release` drops the reservation and leaves them unread.
A program sender's first read makes its mailbox known, as its first post does.
`Inbox`: the caller's senders with unread counts; for an orch caller (`agentname.MatchesRole(role, agentname.RoleOrch)`, so `orch-N` included) the answer also states that its reminders are withheld until `creel-notices` (D16).
`read` and `inbox` from EXTERNAL are refused naming `--wait-reply`.
`Forward(sender, id, receiver)`: holder changes, `From` stays, the caller is appended to `Path`; refused for a message the caller does not hold, one it has read, a forward to itself, to EXTERNAL or to an unknown name.
`Log(filter)`: events filtered by id, from, to and since, with ids, parties, times and subjects and never a body; any caller may run it; a reminder to an orch shows as withheld naming `creel-notices`, read from its `withheld` events (D16).
`Inject(sender, receiver, text, ttl, interrupt)`: a text with a control character is refused; `ttl` defaults to 10 minutes and above 1 hour is refused; the inject is queued per receiver in order, or at the head when `interrupt` is set.
`Interrupt(sender, receiver)`, and `Inject` with `interrupt` set, run in order (D10): the privilege check, the orch refusal, a refusal while `Delivery.Paused` covers the receiver, and a refusal naming the error when `Delivery.Paused` answers one, so an unreadable pause record fails closed (D14), a refusal from any sender but the hold owner while a hold covers it, naming the owner and expiry (the owner's own call passes), and then `Delivery.Interrupt`, at once and bypassing the queue, with `ErrNoRunningTurn` reported to the caller as nothing sent; a refusal queues, interrupts and types nothing.
`Inject` with `interrupt` queues its text at the head after `Delivery.Interrupt` returns nil or `ErrNoRunningTurn` (reported as nothing interrupted), and after any other error returns that error with nothing queued (D10).
Privilege (D9): EXTERNAL, a program sender and an orch strand (`agentname.MatchesRole(role, agentname.RoleOrch)`, so `orch-N` included) are privileged; a non-privileged sender's inject, interrupt or hold on another strand is refused naming `lyx creel post`; any sender but an orch may inject into its own strand.
Orch receivers (D16): inject and interrupt into an orch strand are refused naming `lyx orch distill`, `lyx orch refresh` and the orch's own pane; hold and release on it are accepted and journaled.
`Hold(sender, receiver, for)`: privilege as D9, `for` above 1 hour refused, a hold on a receiver another sender holds refused naming the hold owner and its expiry, a repeat by the owner renews it; `Release` by the owner ends it and journals `released`; `Release` by another sender is refused naming the owner and expiry, and `Release` on a receiver with no live hold answers that nothing was held and journals nothing.
Bound: a privileged sender (EXTERNAL, a program sender or an orch strand) may inject any single-line text without a control character, slash commands included, into any non-orch strand's pane at its next idle, and the interrupt forms stop a running turn there with shuttle's guarded Escape.
The TTL (10 minutes by default, 1 hour at most) bounds staleness only while the receiver is live and unpaused, so an inject queued for a dormant strand waits untyped until the strand is live and idle, and expires when the removed-holder sweep finds the strand gone from every reed state.
What holds the typing back is the pause, hold and orch rules of cards 7 and 9, the pre-write pane guard of card 5 and the refusal of a non-privileged sender.
Bound: holding again renews with no overall cap, so a live privileged owner can mute a receiver while it keeps renewing; an owner that dies mutes it for at most 1 hour.
