## Scope check

The design covers every point the board entry `reed-window-order` asks for, and nothing outside it.

- Overview first for any shed run: D1 adds the `overview` anchor, and the loom-status strand (loom and darn, LLM- or Go-driven, through `lyx loom start`) and the prime's `runs` strand (`lyx batten overview`) move to it.
- Every agent a run spawns, the LLM driver included, in a window after the overview: D2 inserts every own window before the shell, and D4 moves the only two `below-parent` callers (`internal/loomcli/driverspec.go:72`, `internal/orchcli/start.go:79`) to own windows.
  The Go driver's tackler is spawned through shuttle, whose empty anchor already defaults to `own-window` (`internal/shuttleengine/spec.go:217`), so D2 places it too.
- Shell last, named `shell`, replacing VIEW: D3 and D6.
- Attach and automatic switches show the most relevant running agent, never the shell; a new agent takes focus only when the operator is not watching another agent: D5.
  The shell counting as watched is a recorded auto-pick (Q2), a stricter reading of "watching another agent", not a direction the entry rules out.
- Fixed order: D1 and D2 reach it by insert-before-shell; the entry's `move-window -t :99` is offered "for instance" and its rejection is recorded.
- Alt+Up and Alt+Down across windows: D6.
- Overview content (full row list with time and visits, the wait reason in full with an escalation named as waiting on the parent, row detail by scrolling): D7.
- Status panes re-exec on a changed binary, and Windows discussed: D8.
- The `reapplyLayout` zoom flicker: D9.
- The entry asks to drop what `logging-cleanup` already fixed.
  Commit `a90ef61d1` makes the hub watchdog re-stat its binary, release its lock and spawn a successor, so the folded-in watchdog re-exec is correctly out of scope; its `reapplyLayout` still wraps the whole body in `keepZoomLocked`, so D9 stays in.
- `lyx orch watch`, an operator's `lyx reed attach` and other long-lived verbs are left to `stale-binary-daemons`, whose body says only the shared helper, the status panes and the watchdog moved here; no duplication.
- Keeping `AnchorTop`, `AnchorBelowParent` and the stacked layout is out of scope by no entry asking for their removal.

No other board entry asks for window order, reed focus, the checklist frame or status-pane re-exec, so the design duplicates and contradicts none.
