# A Sketch a motif run leaves is followed up, not left lying on the board

`motif-sketch-follow-up` · Note · enhancement, orch, board, stencil

## Seen (operator, 2026-11-27)

A motif run ends by leaving its entry on the board as a Sketch, a note that never runs.
Nothing then follows it up: it waits until someone remembers it.

## Idea

When a motif run lands, the orch is told and puts the Sketch to the operator: promote it to its raddle run now, or say what it waits for.
A Sketch left waiting carries its `depends_on`, so the board shows why.
