### [MEDIUM] D4 leaves two answers of the lifted status policy without an outcome

**Location:** `_lym/discussion/decision-record.md` D4, the paragraph on the new `planindex.Index` method.
**Issue:** `statusFindings` in `internal/planglyph/resolve.go` maps an unpartitioned ambiguous answer to `glyph-ambiguous` and an absent or unknown status to `glyph-rejected`, failing closed.
D4 lifts that policy but names only `citation-symbol-missing` and the package-level `citation-symbol-ambiguous`, so the plan writer cannot tell whether the other two answers pass, fail or error.

### [MEDIUM] D4 states no glyph language and no non-Go behaviour

**Location:** `_lym/discussion/decision-record.md` D4, the glyph token rule.
**Issue:** quarry v0.4.0 `glyph.Parse(lang, s)` requires Go or C#.
D9 limits the plan checks to `language: go`, but D3 and D4 carry no language condition, so a C# or language-none hub's discussion gate runs a rule whose grammar is unstated.

### [MEDIUM] Markdown file anchors read as glyph citations

**Location:** `_lym/discussion/decision-record.md` D4, glyph bullet and Bound.
**Issue:** `docs/overview.md#modules` holds no whitespace or `<`, has a unit holding `/` and `.`, and parses under quarry's Go grammar, which accepts any slash-separated unit.
D3 skips it for its `#`, D4 resolves it, quarry finds no Go member, and the writer is told to mark an existing, correct link as new or removed.
