# The arbiter may make one fix of its own and commit it before it resumes

The arbiter (`internal/shedarbiter`) settles a stop the shed driver's rules and tackler leave, but it could only resume with a goto or hand on.
A stop that needed a small edit went to the orch or the operator, even when the arbiter had read enough to make it.
A loom-engine run's arbiter may now make one fix of its own: it edits the task's code worktree, commits the edit on the task branch, and resumes the run.
Weaver needs this to stand in for Merriam; the change is the arbiter's alone.

## What changed

- `shedarbiter.Deps` gains an optional `Worktree` seam: a worktree snapshot, an ancestor check, a commit log and `Changes`,
  which hashes every uncommitted path, untracked files included.
  The new `internal/shedworktree` package builds it through fabric's read-only reads, and the loom CLI and batten both wire it,
  so tapestry, darn, sampler, pawl and batten arbiters may edit, per the Added after Discussion decisions.
  Batten's seam is over the task worktree, never the prime, which has other writers;
  a batten stop while the task worktree is absent gives a verdict only.
- At spawn the call snapshots the worktree (HEAD, branch, hash of each tracked uncommitted path) and `Changes` into `attempt.json`.
  When the arbiter is done, `readEdits` compares the worktree and `Changes` with those and lists the commits made since.
- A resume is handed on with cause `precondition` when the arbiter switched branch, rewrote history or left uncommitted changes.
  A resume after commits also needs a goto target.
- `envelope.json` and the `lym loom arbiter` output gain `commits`.
  `ArbiterOutcome.Commits` carries them to the driver, which lists them in the resume's re-step settle record (new `commits` key) and in the stop report's attempt line.
  A hand-on's question, the agent's own or the call's, ends by naming the commits for the parent to keep or revert.
- The arbiter stencil gains an `edit_scope` marker, filled from one of two new fragments.
  `loom-fragment-arbiter-fix` states the fix rules and `loom-fragment-arbiter-no-fix` the verdict-only rule.
  The "What you may change" lines and the "never edit a file" and "never run a mutating git command" lines now defer to those rules, and still forbid push, amend, rebase, reset and branch switches.

## Design decisions

The decision record left three questions open; this change settles them as follows.

- **What it may edit:** the task's code, tests and docs in its own worktree.
  The plan, the decision record and PATTERN entries still change only through the repair verbs (`lym loom decision add`, `--amends-plan`, the pattern flags), never by editing the file.
  Nothing under `_lym/` or `.lym/`, and nothing outside the worktree.
  A fix needing more than one focused change, a choice the board entry leaves open, or a scope change stays the parent's, as a hand-on.
- **How an edit is committed and checked:** the arbiter commits it itself, with an `Arbiter-Attempt: <run-id>` trailer, before writing its verdict.
  Go enforces a clean commit on the branch and HEAD it found.
  A resume after commits must name a goto at or before the first row whose gate checks the change, so the run's own rows and gates re-check the fix.
  Go enforces that a goto is present.
  The trailer and which row the goto names are the stencil's instruction, not checked.
- **How the edits show:** the commits appear in the attempt's `envelope.json`, the verb's output, the re-step settle record, the stop report's attempt line and the hand-on question.
  On the task branch they carry the trailer.
- A hand-on keeps the arbiter's commits on the branch rather than reverting them, since lym's code runs no mutating git outside fabric.
  The parent decides, and the question names them.
- No `PATTERN.md` change: the arbiter's commits are an agent's, which `PATTERN-fabric-boundary` does not bind, and the reads go through fabric.

## Tests

- `TestCall_Edits` covers each case: a resume with and without commits, a resume with no goto, the agent's own hand-on, uncommitted changes, a branch switch, rewritten history, a timeout after commits, an unreadable worktree or unreadable uncommitted paths at spawn, an untracked file the arbiter leaves or edits, a read failure after the arbiter, and the no-seam prompt.
- `TestArbiterSeam_Changes` drives the seam's `Changes` over a real checkout: an untracked file, a modified file and a removed file, with exact keys and hashes.
- `TestPrompt` pins the fix rules (worktree and trailer) and the no-fix rules on their own inputs.
- `TestGoDriver_Settle` gains rows for the commits in the stop report's attempt line and in the re-step settle record.
- `TestStencils_TopLevelMarkers` pins both fragments' markers.
  `TestParseSettleRecords` now compares records with `reflect.DeepEqual`, since the record holds a slice.
