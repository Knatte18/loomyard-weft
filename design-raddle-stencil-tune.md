# Tune the raddle stencil and split rubric from the study of every raddle run

`raddle-stencil-tune` · Note · enhancement, docs

## Seen

The study `docs/research/raddle/raddle-split-quality.md` read every raddle run of this hub up to 2026-11-27.
The most common revision is a missing dependency on work already running or released.
Corrections to part count and recipe are about size and cost: too many solid tapestries, or one part too large for one review.
The stencil has no size rule and no recipe or profile rule, and its one cost sentence pushes toward larger parts.

and `contracts/stencils/loom/loom-rubric-raddle-review.md`:
order and dependencies, part count and size, recipe and profile, cut lines, missing or invented parts, wording,
superseded decisions, size estimates, where the entries are filed, and the split perch's rubric.
