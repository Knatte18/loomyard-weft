# An implementer batch gets the files and symbols its cards name, so it starts editing sooner

`implementer-orientation-read` · Note · enhancement, webster, weaver, stencil

## Seen (`docs/research/webster/webster-time-2026-11-08.md`, lever 2)

A Webster fork's first edit came a median 95 s in; orientation was 16.5% of fork time, 12.1 min per row.

## Measured again (`docs/research/webster/gate-time-2026-11-28.md`)

A fork or batch session's first edit comes a mean 136 s in, 13.6 min per row.
The estimated saving is 7 min per row, still resting on the 2026-11-08 assumption that orientation is halved.
The figure gives no Weaver-only number.

## Idea

Hand each batch the file and symbol list its cards name, or have it read them in one batched turn.
Weaver's cold batches are measured on their own before this is built.
