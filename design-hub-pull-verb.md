# One verb that pulls the whole hub, Bolt included, while the orch runs

`hub-pull-verb` · Task · feature, fabric, board · draft

## Seen

Bringing a whole hub up to date takes separate pulls of the prime's warp and weft and of Bolt, and some of them refuse or race while the orch, dobby or a run writes the board.

## Idea

Add one verb that pulls the whole hub (the prime pair, Bolt and each local beam), safe to run while the orch and dobby are live: it takes the locks the board and the landing path take, pulls each side and reports per side what moved or why it stopped.
